My Projects: DOOM and ABHAY

I have created my own AI projects, including DOOM and ABHAY. These projects are important parts of my journey because they represent my creativity, curiosity, and interest in developing intelligent systems. DOOM – My AI Assistant

DOOM is one of my personal AI projects. My vision for DOOM is to create a smart personal assistant that can communicate with users and help them with everyday tasks. I was inspired by the idea of futuristic AI assistants that can answer questions, understand commands, and make technology easier to use.

I wanted DOOM to have features such as a chat interface, voice interaction, memory, camera-based vision, and 3D model creation. My goal was to explore how different technologies could work together to create a useful and interactive assistant. I have worked with web development technologies, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and explored how a backend server can connect a website to an AI service.

While developing DOOM, I learned that building an AI assistant is not just about writing code. It also involves designing a user-friendly interface, connecting APIs, handling errors, testing features, and improving the overall experience. Sometimes, the code does not work as expected, or a server produces an error. However, these challenges provide opportunities to learn and understand programming more deeply.

I want to continue improving my knowledge and developing my ideas step by step. For me, DOOM is more than just a project; it is an opportunity to explore the future of personal AI assistants and understand how intelligent technology can become part of everyday life.
